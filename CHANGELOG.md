## 0.1.0

- First version: the **Glowing cores** dice system for Dice So Nice 5, with d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 and d100 that hold a glowing core of light inside the die.
- 16 glows: Ember Heart, Arcane Vortex, Pocket Nebula, Storm Cell, Heartstone, Eclipse, Frost Lattice, Lava Lamp, Plasma Globe, Aurora, Will-o'-Wisps, Arcane Rings, Bubbling Potion, Twin Helix, Radiant Sun and Fairy Lights.
- System options per die type in Dice So Nice's own dialog: glow, the glow's own or your own colours, intensity, core size, animation speed, pulse (steady, breathe, heartbeat, flicker), rim light, how dark the die body gets, and bloom with realistic lighting. Everyone at the table sees your glow on your rolls.
- Client setting **Glow quality** (low, medium, high).
